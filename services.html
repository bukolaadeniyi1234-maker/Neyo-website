Division 01

Neyo Crop Farming

Our crop farming division produces some of Nigeria's most prized staples. We cultivate yams, pepper, and maize on nutrient-rich farmland using both traditional knowledge and modern agronomic practices.

  • Premium white and yellow yam tubers
  • Fresh and sun-dried pepper varieties (tatashe, rodo, shombo)
  • Sweet and field maize — cob and shelled
  • Seasonal availability with pre-order options

Division 02

Neyo Poultry

Our poultry facility is modern, biosecure, and managed to the highest standards. We rear broiler chickens for meat and layer hens for egg production — all in climate-controlled houses with optimal nutrition programs.

  • Live broiler chickens (ready at 6–8 weeks)
  • Fresh table eggs — grades A and B
  • Point-of-lay pullets (seasonal)
  • Bulk and retail supply available

Division 03

Neyo Fish

Our fish ponds produce fresh, healthy catfish and tilapia year-round. Raised in clean water with quality feed and zero harmful chemicals, Neyo fish is the choice of discerning buyers who know the difference.

  • Live and dressed Clarias catfish
  • Fresh Tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus)
  • Fingerlings for fish farmers (bulk orders)
  • Same-day delivery on fresh orders

Division 04

Neyo Snails

Giant African land snails (Archachatina marginata) are one of Nigeria's most nutritious and delicious delicacies. Neyo Snails division produces well-grown snails in a clean, controlled heliciculture environment.

  • Live giant African land snails
  • High in protein, iron, and omega-3
  • Popular in restaurants and home cooking
  • Available in dozens, half-bags, and full bags
Shop Snails
Division 05

Neyo Pig Farming

Our pig farm operates in a dedicated, biosecure facility. We raise pigs on a balanced diet, producing healthy weaners for other farmers and fresh dressed pork for the food industry and direct consumers.

  • Healthy weaner pigs (4–8 weeks)
  • Fattener pigs (ready for slaughter)
  • Fresh dressed pork — whole, half, and cuts
  • Supply to restaurants, abattoirs, and processors
