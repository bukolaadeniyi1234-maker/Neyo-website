Our Story

Built on the Land. Built for Nigeria.

Neyo Integrated Farm Services Nigeria Limited was founded with a singular vision: to produce and deliver the highest quality agricultural products directly from Nigerian soil to Nigerian tables. We believe in the power of integrated farming — where crops, poultry, fish, snails, and pigs work in ecological harmony on the same land.

Through years of innovation and investment in modern farming infrastructure, Neyo has grown into a multi-division agricultural enterprise serving households, restaurants, hotels, and wholesalers across Nigeria.

Our Mission

Feed Nigeria with fresh, affordable, and traceable farm produce.

Our Vision

Become Africa's most trusted integrated farm services brand.

Our Values

Quality, sustainability, integrity, and community impact.

Our Promise

Farm-to-table freshness in every product, every delivery.
